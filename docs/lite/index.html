Plants screened together (WSCR)

How it is calculated. SCR = short-circuit MVA ÷ plant MW. WSCR = Σ(SCMVAᵢ × Pᵢ) ÷ (ΣPᵢ)², assuming the plants in a group are fully interacting (ERCOT/NERC). Max size (largest plant before a weak flag) = SCMVA ÷ weak threshold. Condenser MVA = max(0, MW × target SCR − SCMVA) × (X″d + Xt), assuming the condenser is at the point of interconnection. Default flag bands follow HVDC planning practice (IEEE Std 1204-1997); there is no universal weak-grid threshold.

Screening only. Results do not replace interconnection studies, detailed positive-sequence or EMT analysis, or the transmission provider's requirements. SCR-based metrics assume grid-following inverters and do not replace the inverter manufacturer's minimum SCR. Cost screening excludes network upgrades, which are often the largest interconnection cost.
